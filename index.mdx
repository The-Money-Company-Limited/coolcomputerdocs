Cool Computers gives you a persistent Linux computer with a name. Choose `name.cool.computer` and that name is the whole identity: the public address on the web, the `name@mail.cool.computer` inbox, and the machine your agents work inside.

It is your computer on the internet, not another AI app builder. Install what you need, run services, close the tab. A sleeping computer costs nothing and forgets nothing. The first request to `name.cool.computer` wakes it and reaches its service.

## What persists

The computer's writable disk survives sleep and restore: code, installed packages, databases, configuration, and enabled services. Its name, address, and inbox stay with it for life. What does not persist is what never persists on any rebooted machine: running processes, RAM, and `/tmp`.
