Cool Computers puts what you and your agent make online under a name. A computer named `bakery` has the public address `bakery.cool.computer` and the inbox `bakery@mail.cool.computer`. It starts as a published site with no VM, and gets an always-on VM when the work needs a server, a bot, or a schedule.

A computer stays on your account until you delete it. One made by `cool publish` without signing in is deleted, with its site, after 24 hours unless you [claim it](/use/cli#publish-a-site). Its files, name, address, and inbox stay with it. Requests to its public address reach its published files first, then the HTTP port you expose.

## Other ways to connect

- Run [`ssh cool.computer`](/agents/ssh) to open your account without installing the CLI.
- [Email a task](/use/email) to the inbox attached to a computer.
