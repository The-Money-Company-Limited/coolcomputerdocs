Cool Computers gives you a persistent Linux computer with a name. A computer named `bakery` has the public address `bakery.cool.computer` and the inbox `bakery@mail.cool.computer`.

Its writable disk, name, address, inbox, and configured service remain while it sleeps. A request to its public address can wake it and reach the service.

## Other ways to connect

- Run `ssh cool.computer` to open your account without installing the CLI.
- [Email a task](/use/email) to the inbox attached to a computer.
