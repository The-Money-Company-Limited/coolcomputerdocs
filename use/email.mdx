Every computer has an inbox at `name@mail.cool.computer`. The inbox stays with the computer until you delete it.

## Send a task

Write a direct instruction from the email address on your Cool Computers account:

```text
To: bakery@mail.cool.computer
Subject: Menu update

Add today's sourdough to the menu and publish it.
```

The agent inside the computer works in `/home/runtime` and replies to the thread with its result. Reply to the same thread when you want it to continue.

## Who can start a task

Only mail from your verified account address can start work, and only when it carries a DKIM signature from that address's own domain. Other messages reach the inbox but don't run a task. Each email's `intake_outcome` and `intake_detail`, in `runtime-env email inbox --json` and the HTTP API, say why and what to do next.

### Turn on DKIM for your own domain

New Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 domains don't sign mail with their own domain until an admin turns it on: Google signs as `*.gappssmtp.com` and Microsoft as `*.onmicrosoft.com`. Your email then doesn't start a task (`rejected_owner_domain_unsigned`), and Cool Computers replies with these steps, at most once an hour per computer.

To check, send a test email to another mailbox and open the received copy's original headers (in Gmail, More → Show original). Its `Authentication-Results` must show `dkim=pass` with `header.d=` set to your own domain; a `d=` of `…gappssmtp.com` or `…onmicrosoft.com` means domain signing is off.

- **Google Workspace:** admin.google.com → Apps → Google Workspace → Gmail → Authenticate email → select your domain → Generate new record → add the TXT record it shows at `google._domainkey.<domain>` at your DNS provider → back in the console, click Start authentication. It can take up to 48 hours.
- **Microsoft 365:** security.microsoft.com → Email & collaboration → Policies & rules → Threat policies → Email authentication settings → DKIM → select your domain → add the two CNAME records shown → Enable.
- **Other providers:** turn on DKIM signing for your domain in your email provider's settings.

## Follow a task

Open [Chats](https://www.cool.computer/app/chats) to follow the conversation started by your email. The reply also arrives in the original email thread. Reply to that thread to continue the task.

## Work with mail inside the computer

```sh
runtime-env email address
runtime-env email inbox --json
runtime-env email read <email-id> --json
runtime-env email send --to you@example.com --subject "Report" --text "Done."
```

The HTTP API and [MCP connector](/use/agents) also support reading and sending mail. Obtain approval for the exact recipient and content before sending. The `cool` CLI does not expose inbox operations.
