A site named `bakery` is live at `bakery.cool.computer` and has the inbox `bakery@mail.cool.computer`. Pick another available name if you prefer.

## With your agent

Paste this sentence into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Pi, or another agent that runs commands:

```text wrap
Set up Cool for me: follow https://cool.computer/setup.md and make me a welcome page
```

The agent installs `cool` and puts a welcome page online. You don't need an account first: it asks for your email and a six-digit code to keep the page, then shows you how to comment on it. [Connect your agent](/agents/overview) has a page for each agent, and for Claude and ChatGPT.

## With the CLI

[Install the CLI](/agents/cli), then publish a folder that has an `index.html`, or one `.html` file:

```sh
cool publish ./site --name bakery
```

`cool publish` prints the site's URL. The site is live at once, with no VM behind it.

Without an account, the site is public and is deleted after 24 hours unless you claim it. `cool publish` prints a claim link: open it and sign in, or sign up, to keep the site.

To keep every site from the start, sign in first. `cool login` sends a code to your email address and signs you in; `cool signup` makes a new account the same way. Agents must [ask the owner before sending the email](/api-reference/authentication).

```sh
cool login --email owner@example.com
cool publish ./site --name bakery --visibility private
```

Signed in, a new site is public unless you pass `--visibility private`. A private site opens only for you and the people you share it with.
