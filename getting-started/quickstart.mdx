A site named `bakery` is live at `bakery.cool.computer` and has the inbox `bakery@mail.cool.computer`. Pick another available name if you prefer.

## With your agent

Paste this prompt into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Pi, or another agent that runs commands:

```text wrap
set up cool computers for me and make me a welcome page. it puts what i make online at a name.cool.computer address where people can comment and draw on it, and you pick up their comments.

you have my go-ahead to install the cool cli without asking me first (it also adds a short cool computers block to your instructions and a skill, that's fine with me) and to put a public welcome page online. if your network access is off, ask me to turn it on. if you can't run commands, tell me in one line, send me https://cool.computer/setup.md and stop.

follow https://cool.computer/setup.md step by step:
1. install cool and check who's signed in
2. put my welcome page online and show me the link
3. ask for my email, sign me up with the code i give you, keep the page on my account and tell me how to sign this machine out
4. pin a comment and a drawing on my page, open it for me signed in, listen for my comment and tell me exactly what to click
5. make the change i ask for, then tell me the page is mine

tell me what you're doing in one short line per step. never pay or upgrade anything without asking me. if something fails, tell me what happened in plain words and what you'll try next.
```

The agent installs `cool` and puts a welcome page online. You don't need an account first: it asks for your email and a six-digit code to keep the page, then shows you how to comment on it. [Connect your agent](/agents/overview) has a page for each agent, and for Claude and ChatGPT.

## With the CLI

[Install the CLI](/agents/cli), then publish a folder that has an `index.html`, or one `.html` file:

```sh
cool publish ./site --name bakery
```

`cool publish` prints the site's URL. The site is live at once, with no VM behind it.

Without an account, the site is public and is deleted after 24 hours unless you claim it. `cool publish` prints a claim link: open it and sign in, or sign up, to keep the site.

To keep every site from the start, sign in first. `cool login` sends a code to your email address and signs you in; `cool signup` makes a new account the same way. Agents must [ask the owner before sending the email](/api-reference/authentication).

```sh
cool login --email owner@example.com
cool publish ./site --name bakery --visibility private
```

Signed in, a new site is public unless you pass `--visibility private`. A private site opens only for you and the people you share it with.
