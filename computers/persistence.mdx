---
title: "What persists"
description: "See which parts of a computer remain when it sleeps or moves through cold storage."
---

Sleep changes whether a computer is running. It does not create a different computer.

## Writable disk

The complete writable disk persists. This includes code, installed packages, databases, configuration, and local state.

## Identity

The computer keeps its name, `name.cool.computer` address, and `name@mail.cool.computer` inbox.

## Configured service

The configured service returns when the computer wakes. A request to the public address can trigger the wake and reach the service.

## Deletion is different

Sleep and cold storage preserve the computer. Deletion removes it. Read the current [privacy policy](https://www.cool.computer/privacy) for the published deletion and retention terms.
