## Add the connector

<Card title="Add to Claude" icon="/images/agents/claude.svg" href="https://claude.ai/customize/connectors?modal=add-custom-connector&connectorName=Cool%20Computers&connectorUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fapi.cool.computer%2Fmcp">
  Opens Claude with Cool Computers filled in. Choose **Add**, then sign in, or sign up.
</Card>

The connector works on claude.ai and in the Claude desktop app. On the Free plan you can add one custom connector.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Add it by hand">
    1. Open **Customize**, then **Connectors**.
    2. Choose **Add custom connector**.
    3. Name it Cool Computers and enter `https://api.cool.computer/mcp`.
    4. If Claude asks how people sign in, choose **Sign in now**, and keep **Use Claude's published identity** as the OAuth client.
    5. Choose **Add**, then sign in to Cool Computers, or sign up.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="On a Team or Enterprise plan">
    An Owner adds the connector for the organization from **Organization settings**, **Connectors**, **Add**, **Custom**, **Web**. [This link](https://claude.ai/admin-settings/connectors?modal=add-custom-connector&connectorName=Cool%20Computers&connectorUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fapi.cool.computer%2Fmcp) opens that dialog filled in.

    Each member then chooses **Connect** in **Customize**, **Connectors** and signs in to Cool Computers for themselves.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Paste this

In a new chat, ask:

```text wrap
make me a welcome page and put it on cool.computer
```

<Card title="Open Claude" icon="/images/agents/claude.svg" href="https://claude.ai/new?q=make%20me%20a%20welcome%20page%20and%20put%20it%20on%20cool.computer">
  Opens a new chat in Claude with the request typed in.
</Card>

Claude publishes the page with the connector and gives you its link.

## Check it works

Ask Claude:

```text
List my Cool Computers sites and computers. Don't change anything.
```

It answers with your list. If it doesn't, open **Customize**, **Connectors** and choose **Connect** on Cool Computers.
