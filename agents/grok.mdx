## Add the connector

1. Open [grok.com](https://grok.com), then **Plugins**.
2. Choose **New Connector**, then **Custom**.
3. Name it Cool Computers and enter this URL:

   ```text
   https://api.cool.computer/mcp
   ```

4. Sign in to Cool Computers, or sign up.

## Paste this

In a new chat, ask:

```text wrap
make me a welcome page and put it on cool.computer
```

Grok publishes the page with the connector and gives you its link.

## Check it works

Ask Grok:

```text
List my Cool Computers sites and computers. Don't change anything.
```

It answers with your list. If it doesn't, open **Plugins** and check that Cool Computers is connected.
