## Add the connector

1. Open [Grok Connectors](https://grok.com/connectors).
2. Choose **New Connector**, then search the catalog for Cool Computers and choose it.
3. Sign in to Cool Computers, or sign up.

On Grok Business and Enterprise, an admin adds the connector in the xAI console first. Members then connect it at [grok.com/connectors](https://grok.com/connectors).

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Add it by hand">
    1. Open [Grok Connectors](https://grok.com/connectors).
    2. Choose **New Connector**, then **Custom**.
    3. Name it Cool Computers and enter this URL:

       ```text
       https://api.cool.computer/mcp
       ```

    4. Sign in to Cool Computers, or sign up.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Paste this

In a new chat, ask:

```text wrap
make me a welcome page and put it on cool.computer
```

Grok publishes the page with the connector and gives you its link.

## Check it works

Ask Grok:

```text
List my Cool Computers sites and computers. Don't change anything.
```

It answers with your list. If it doesn't, open [Grok Connectors](https://grok.com/connectors) and check that Cool Computers is connected.
